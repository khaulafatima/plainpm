# PlainPM

PlainPM tracks project work and the context needed to continue it across sessions.

## Language

**Handoff**:
A session's record of accomplishments, unfinished work, and context for whoever continues it. A handoff belongs to the session and may cover multiple PRDs or backlog issues.
_Avoid_: PRD-owned handoff

**Handoff association**:
The relationship identifying PRDs recorded as context for a session, whether through its issues or planning work. A handoff can cover multiple PRDs, and later changes in issue ownership do not erase that historical context.

**Active handoff**:
A handoff whose continuation notes still require attention.

**Resolved handoff**:
A handoff whose continuation notes have been addressed or explicitly dismissed. Resolution concerns those notes, not the completion of every referenced issue.
